Smart Outdoor Lights turns your outdoor lights on at dusk and off at dawn – at the right time all year round.

The app calculates the sun's position for your Homey's location, so it automatically adapts to the seasons, midnight sun and polar night. Choose whether the lights should turn on at sunset, dusk or when it is dark, and fine-tune it to the minute.

Features:
- Pick lights by room and type (lights, sockets, switches)
- Optional night break, e.g. off at 23:30 and back on at 06:00 if it is still dark
- Optional light sensor to switch on earlier on dark, cloudy days
- Status device showing lights state, darkness, sun altitude, next on/off and estimated outdoor lux
- Flow cards: lights switched, "it is dark outside", pause automation
